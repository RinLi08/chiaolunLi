    ABOUT

    Hi, I’m Chiao-Lun Li, a 2D Artist and Animator with over 10 years of experience in animation and game art. My work spans TV series, films, commercials, character animation, storyboarding, character design, and game VFX. From expressive character performances to dynamic visual effects, I enjoy bringing ideas to life through movement, storytelling, and diverse visual styles. Beyond digital art, I also explore bringing my original characters into the physical world through metalsmithing, clay sculpting, and wax carving—creating unique wearable art and sculptural objects.
